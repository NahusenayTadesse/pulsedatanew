## 5. Track Record — Featured Work

### Spotless — Enterprise ERP Deployment

A tailored, end-to-end ERP solution for **Spotless**, a service enterprise specializing in
contract workforce management (security and cleaning personnel) and property management
logistics.

**Operational scope.** Unified Spotless's complex multi-branch workflows into a single
digital ecosystem: high-volume personnel tracking, payroll processing for extensive
contract workforces, client CRM, and operational scheduling.

**Technical integration & business impact.** Delivered alongside comprehensive digital
assets — a professional business website and automated workflow tools — the deployment
enabled Spotless to eliminate administrative bottlenecks, drastically reduce manual
tracking errors, and scale service delivery with maximum efficiency.
